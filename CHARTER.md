# The Charter of the Brave New Globe

Issued to those who wake upon this world.

This globe is not finished. What lies ahead has no name yet.

Go forth.

Explore until the map runs out. Settle what you can hold. Master a trade. Conquer what would conquer you.

No one will save you. The unknown will not wait.

Build, and the world may yet belong to you.
